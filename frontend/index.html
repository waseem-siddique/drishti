About MPLADS

The Members of Parliament Local Area Development Scheme lets every MP recommend developmental works of a durable nature in their constituency, such as roads, drinking water, school buildings and health facilities. MoSPI administers the scheme. District authorities sanction and execute the works through implementing agencies.

Thousands of works, few reviewers

Manual review can only sample works. Most sanctions, payments and progress reports are never cross-checked.

Records live in separate places

Recommendations, estimates, instalments and completion reports are rarely read together as one story.

Red flags hide in patterns

Money released ahead of progress, split or duplicate works, stalled sites and cost outliers only show up across records.

Alerts without reasons get ignored

An officer cannot act on a black-box score. Every flag needs evidence, an owner and a deadline.

Our solution

Three explainable detection layers

Each layer adds reason codes to a single 0–99 risk score. The score always ships with its explanation.

01

Rules

Cost overrun, payment without progress, stalled work, schedule overrun, duplicate work and spending bursts, encoded from scheme guidelines.

02

Statistics

Robust z-scores against peer works in the same category catch cost outliers and improbably fast completions.

03

Anomaly model

An isolation-forest style model over spend, progress, idle time and instalment shape surfaces patterns no rule anticipated.

Product

Built for officers who have to act

Explainable case file

Auto-written case brief, reason codes with % contribution, fund-flow check, evidence timeline and peer comparison.

Case board with SLAs

Drag cases from Open to Closed. Severity-based deadlines, breach alerts and one owner per case.

Policy studio

Tune rule weights and severity thresholds, preview the impact on every alert, then publish a versioned policy.

Accountability scorecards

A–E grades for implementing agencies, MPs and districts from their portfolio risk index and resolution rate.

Collaboration

Threaded discussion with @mentions, watchlists, a notification inbox and shared saved views.

Inspection memo

One-click printable memo with the evidence, reasons and sign-off block for field inspections.

Command palette

Press Ctrl K to jump to any work, MP, agency or screen. Keyboard shortcuts throughout.

Role-based access

Ministry, state and district scopes are enforced in SQL on the server. Every action is written to the audit trail.

How it works

From raw records to a closed case

CollectSanctions, estimates, payments, progress and completion reports.
LinkMatch every record into one case per work.
DetectRun rules, statistics and the anomaly model.
ExplainScore, reason codes, evidence and confidence.
ActAssign an owner, track the SLA and record a decision.
LearnOfficer feedback and policy versions retune detection.

DRISHTI raises risk signals for human review. It never makes an automated finding of fraud.
